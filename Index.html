Our story

AETHER was born from the soft light of dawn over the olive groves. We believe that luxury should feel gentle, not imposing — like a warm breeze.

Our kitchen celebrates the subtle tones of heirloom vegetables, wild herbs, and sustainable seafood. Every plate is a quiet composition.
